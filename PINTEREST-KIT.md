## Pin posting rules (important)
- **Disclose affiliate links:** Include `#ad` or `#affiliate` and “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases” where appropriate.
- **Be honest:** Use real product names/descriptions; don’t promise fake discounts.
- **No spam:** Post quality pins consistently; don’t dump dozens of identical links at once.
- **Use your own images/graphics:** Create original pin graphics or use allowed product imagery per program rules. Avoid copying random copyrighted images.
- **Link destination:** Use your affiliate link or a relevant page on your site.
